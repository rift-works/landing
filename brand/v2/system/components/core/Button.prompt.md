The system's button — mono, uppercase, square corners, no shadow, and the label is always a verb.

```jsx
<Button>Start a project</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">See the approach</Button>
<Button variant="accent" size="sm">Enviar</Button>
```

- `accent` is Oxide-filled and counts as the view's one accent element. Do not put two on a screen.
- Hover darkens or inverts; nothing lifts, scales or shadows. Press is colour only.
- Never write "Submit" or "Click here" — `ENVIAR`, `NUEVO PROYECTO`, `START A PROJECT`.
